Pet Home (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy.   • Location data (with your consent)

  • Device information (device ID, operating system, app version)
  • Usage data and analytics

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Personalize and improve your experience
  • Display targeted advertisements
  • Comply with legal obligations

  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen advertisements displayed between content or during transitions
  • Banner Ads: Rectangular advertisements displayed at the top or bottom of the screen
  • Native Ads: Advertisements that match the form and function of the app interface
  • Rewarded Interstitial Ads: Premium interstitial ads with optional rewards

To provide personalized ads, our advertising partners may use:
