Who We Are

Our Story

Founded with a passion for pets and technology, Pet Home has grown into a comprehensive pet care platform serving thousands of pet owners across China and beyond.

Pet Home (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd was established with a clear mission: to leverage technology to improve the lives of pets and their owners. We recognized that pet care, while essential, often lacked the convenience and efficiency that modern pet owners deserved.

Today, we combine cutting-edge mobile technology with traditional pet care services to create an ecosystem that addresses every aspect of pet ownership. From booking grooming appointments to tracking vaccinations, from finding nearby pet-friendly venues to connecting with fellow pet lovers - we've got you covered.

Our apps are available on Google Play and the App Store, providing seamless access to our services for millions of pet owners worldwide. We believe that every pet deserves the best care, and every pet owner deserves the convenience of modern technology.
